# Code of conduct

Tacho contributors must provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment.
Participate with empathy, give actionable technical feedback, respect privacy
and assume good faith while remaining accountable for impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure
of private information and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude people
whose behaviour is harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
